Police Enforcement Dashboard

Australian Road Safety Enforcement Dashboard

This dashboard visualizes traffic enforcement data across Australia. It tracks traffic offenses, detection methods, demographics, and trends over time. Click a topic in the menu above (such as Seatbelts or Mobile Phone) to filter and view the interactive charts.

Data Sources & Methodology

1. Main Dataset: Road Safety Enforcement Data

This is the core dataset used for all the charts on this dashboard. It contains records of official traffic fines, arrests, and legal charges across different states and territories.

  • Source: Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE) / DITRDCSA
  • Portals: Australia Road Safety Data Hub | Infrastructure Data Catalogue
  • Direct Files: Excel Sheet | CSV File

2. Normalization Data: Driver Licence Holders

Because highly populated states naturally have more total offenses, we use this second dataset to calculate a fair comparative metric: offenses per 10,000 licence holders. This prevents raw population numbers from skewing the data.

  • Source: BITRE Australian Infrastructure and Transport Statistics Yearbook
  • Links: Yearbook Publication Page | Direct PDF Document

3. Definitions: Data Dictionary

This reference document is used to verify metadata schemas and ensure fields like "Fines" and "Charges" match standardized classifications.

  • Link: Road Safety Enforcement Data Dictionary PDF
How to use: Adjust the dropdown choices below to narrow down data by location, year, age, or enforcement method. You can hover over specific sections of the charts to reveal detailed breakdowns.You can also click on specific lines or colored regions, or click on the symbol or color on the legend to highlight that line or region to view in more clearly.
Data Note:
(1) For chart titled "Jurisdiction (Normalized by License Holders)"", rate-based charts utilize normalization data calculated per 10,000 licence holders to enable an accurate baseline comparison between states. However, it can only be filtered by years and jurisdiction, so filtering by any other criteria will not display any data for the chart.
(2) Location and age group data is only collected after year 2023, so filtering by years before 2023 will not display any data for the two charts.
